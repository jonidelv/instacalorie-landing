Questions

Good to know.

How does Instacalorie work?

You take one photo of your meal. The app recognizes the food, estimates the portion, and returns calories plus protein, fat, and carbs. Adjust anything you want, then save.

Do I have to weigh food or search a database?

No. There is no database to search and nothing to weigh. A single photo is the entire input.

How accurate are the numbers?

Estimates are based on the photo and get closer to you the more you log and confirm. It is built for fast, consistent everyday tracking.

Which devices will it support?

Instacalorie launches on iPhone. iOS is the focus at launch.
