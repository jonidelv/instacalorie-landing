1. What Instacalorie does

Instacalorie lets you photograph your meals, photograph nutrition labels, and scan product barcodes. Our systems, including artificial intelligence, analyze each photo to identify the food and estimate its calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, and related nutritional information. Barcodes are looked up in Open Food Facts, an open product database made available under the Open Database License, and the product's name and listed facts are shown to you as that database lists them, then scored by the same kind of system. The photo and the data derived from it are saved to your account so the Service can build your daily, weekly, and monthly nutrition history.

The Service can also suggest daily goals from what you tell it about your aim, and it includes Intelligence, a chat that answers questions about your own meals, goals, and weight. Intelligence is described in section 3.
